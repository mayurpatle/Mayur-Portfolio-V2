  • 02

    GeoPulse

    LOCATION TRACKING · SPATIAL INGESTION3 services · designed for 1M drivers

    A location-tracking and spatial-ingestion engine for GPS pings from moving drivers. Pings are encoded to H3 cells and partitioned by geography in Kafka, then split across two consumer groups: a hot path into Redis for fast nearby queries and a history path into Cassandra for route replay and cell occupancy. Deduplication on the hot path, idempotent upserts on the history path, and a circuit breaker when Redis is down.
